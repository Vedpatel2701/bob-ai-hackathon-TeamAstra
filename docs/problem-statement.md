# Problem Statement

## Background

Global supply chains depend on coordinated logistics networks involving hundreds of vehicles, warehouses, ports, and suppliers operating simultaneously. Operations teams must monitor shipment status, manage fleet allocation, respond to disruptions, and meet delivery deadlines — all under conditions of high variability including weather events, traffic congestion, port delays, and supplier reliability issues.

## The Problem

Supply chain operations centers currently lack a unified decision-support system that can:

1. **Predict disruption risk before it occurs** — rather than reacting after a delay has already happened
2. **Explain why a specific shipment is at risk** — rather than providing a black-box alert with no actionable context
3. **Recommend optimal fleet assignments** — rather than relying on manual dispatcher judgment under time pressure
4. **Enable rapid scenario planning** — to evaluate how conditions (weather, traffic, vehicle availability) affect outcomes before committing resources

Operations managers responsible for 200–500 active shipments spend significant time reactively triaging incidents because no integrated tool correlates operational signals — weather severity, port congestion, traffic level, warehouse delays, supplier risk, vehicle utilization — into a clear risk picture.

## Who is Affected

- **Logistics operations managers** responsible for 24/7 fleet dispatch and shipment monitoring
- **Supply chain analysts** who must produce delay risk reports and root-cause analyses
- **Fleet coordinators** responsible for vehicle-to-shipment assignment under capacity and availability constraints
- **Operations executives** who need KPI dashboards showing on-time rates, cost, and risk exposure

## Why It Matters

- A single delayed critical shipment can trigger downstream production line stoppages, with costs ranging from thousands to hundreds of thousands of dollars
- Reactive dispatching leads to suboptimal fleet utilization, increasing per-shipment operating cost
- Without root-cause transparency, operations teams make repeated allocation mistakes because the underlying risk patterns are not surfaced
- Manual fleet assignment under time pressure leads to capacity violations, deadline misses, and underutilized vehicles

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Current logistics tools typically provide:
- **Static dashboards** that show shipment status after delays occur rather than predicting them
- **Rule-based alert systems** that fire on single threshold breaches without understanding multi-factor risk combinations
- **Separate route optimization tools** that do not incorporate live risk signals or disruption predictions
- **No natural-language interface** — requiring operators to navigate complex forms when they need fast answers

None of these tools combine predictive ML, explainability, optimization, and conversational AI into a single decision-support workflow.
